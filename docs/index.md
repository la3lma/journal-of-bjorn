### Welcome to My Journal

**I'm Bjorn, and this is a record of my curiosity.**

Before you dive in, a quick disclaimer: this is a journal in the personal sense, not a scientific one. While I've chosen a formal, academic layout for these entries, they are essentially extended diary notes rather than peer-reviewed research.

Why the formal format? Quite simply, I like how papers read. It helps me organize my thoughts. My process usually looks like this:

- I get obsessed with a topic.
- I dive into a rabbit hole using searcj and AI assistants.
- I synthesize those dialogues into the short papers you see here.

**A Note on Quality**

These are snapshots of what I found fascinating on any given day. They haven't been vetted, and because they are AI-augmented, they may contain hallucinations or errors. Think of them as intellectual artifacts rather than absolute truths. The dates are not very precise. They represent a best guess based on incomplete information about when the document was created. As a general rule, it was created at or before the date listed, but probably not in every case.

If something sparks your interest, or if you spot a mistake, I'd be delighted to talk. I wrote these because I care about these topics, and I'd love to find others who do too.

**A Note on the "Ø"**

One last thing: You might see me toggle between Bjørn and Bjorn. I have no desire to create typographical hurdles for those of you without a Norwegian keyboard. Ever since the legendary Björn Borg made the "Bjorn" pronunciation an international standard, the simplified version has become essentially inescapable anyway, so I've decided to just lean into it. Call me Bjørn, call me Bjorn—I will answer, and I will love you in any case.
